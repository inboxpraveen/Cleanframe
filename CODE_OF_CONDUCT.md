# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CleanFrame to be a place where anyone can turn up with a messy CSV and get help,
regardless of experience, background, or identity. Everyone taking part in this project —
issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews — is expected to treat other people with
respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and specific. Review code, not people.
- Assume the other person is doing their best with the information they had.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that contributors may decline to
  make one. Say why, once, and move on.
- Redact real data before pasting it into an issue. Someone else's personal data is not
  yours to share.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal or political attacks, insults, sustained disruption, publishing
someone's private information, unwelcome sexual attention, and any other conduct that
would reasonably make someone unwilling to take part.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer via the contact on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/inboxpraveen). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed from the project.

Maintainers will respond as they judge appropriate: a private word, a public correction,
removal of a comment, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
